Balanced
Higher levels give the optimizer more search effort and improve the odds of finding a better set. With the genetic algorithm, thoroughness sets how many complete gear sets are evaluated per generation and how many generations without improvement to wait. With simulated annealing, it sets the number of random-swap cycles (thoroughness squared).
